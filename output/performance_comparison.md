# Out-of-Sample Portfolio Performance

The portfolios use fixed unconstrained global minimum-variance weights estimated
from the first 70% of the common sample. Performance is evaluated over the
remaining 30% of observations. Returns and volatility are annualised using 252
trading days. The reported Sharpe ratio assumes a zero risk-free rate.

| Covariance estimator | Annualised return | Annualised volatility | Maximum drawdown | Sharpe ratio (zero RF) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Sample covariance | 4.41% | 16.47% | -16.92% | 0.268 |
| Ledoit-Wolf linear shrinkage (identity target) | **4.71%** | **16.39%** | **-16.75%** | **0.287** |
| Nonlinear shrinkage | 4.47% | 16.46% | -16.85% | 0.272 |

## Interpretation

Linear shrinkage produced the lowest out-of-sample volatility, reducing annualised
volatility from 16.47% to 16.39%. It also generated the least severe maximum
drawdown and the highest zero-risk-free-rate Sharpe ratio. Nonlinear shrinkage
produced only marginal improvements relative to the sample covariance estimator.

Because this is a minimum-variance exercise, volatility is the principal outcome.
The slightly higher realised returns should not be interpreted as a return forecast
generated by shrinkage: the portfolio construction procedure estimates covariance,
not expected returns. The differences are small and should be treated as an
illustration rather than conclusive evidence of superior performance.
